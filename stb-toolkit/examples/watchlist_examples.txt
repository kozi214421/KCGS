## Watchlist Management Tips
1. Limit to 30-50 symbols maximum
2. Organize by priority/tier
3. Review weekly, remove non-performers
4. Add new symbols showing momentum
5. Balance across sectors
6. Focus on liquid stocks (>1M daily volume)
7. Consider market cap requirements
8. Update based on market conditions

## Symbol Selection Criteria
✓ Average Daily Volume > 1M shares
✓ Market Cap > $100M
✓ Price > $1.00
✓ Options available (for enrichment data)
✓ Reasonable spread (< 1%)
✓ Trending or high volatility
✓ Sector diversification

## Symbols to Generally Avoid
✗ Penny stocks (< $1)
✗ Low volume (< 500k daily)
✗ Wide spreads (> 2%)
✗ Recently IPO'd (< 3 months)
✗ Highly manipulated stocks
✗ Illiquid ETFs
✗ OTC stocks
